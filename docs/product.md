# Product

## Positioning

**VutaDex is a persistent online American-football universe where you can run a franchise as a GM, call plays as a coach, or take control on the X/O field as a player.**

The X/O tactical field is the product's visual identity. VutaDex is not trying to reproduce Madden's 3D presentation. Development effort goes into football intelligence, multiplayer decisions, persistent history and a fast browser experience.

## Modes

### GM

Roster construction, scouting, draft, contracts, trades, free agency, staff and long-term franchise direction.

### Coach

Personnel, formations, play selection, audibles, tempo, timeouts, fourth-down decisions and situational strategy. Coach Mode is the first playable release target.

### Player

Direct post-snap control begins with QB, RB and MLB. Server validation and latency compensation are required before expanding positions.

## The Dex

Every durable result becomes history: players, teams, seasons, games, drafts, transactions, awards, records, championships and rivalries. Generated players retain immutable IDs even after retirement or team changes.

## Competitive distinction

Management depth is valuable, but the product wedge is playable football. GM and franchise systems feed a live game humans can coach, watch and eventually play directly.
